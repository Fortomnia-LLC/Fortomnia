## Common Help Topics

### Sign-in problems

Confirm that you are using the email associated with your account and that your device has an active internet connection. If email confirmation is enabled, complete the confirmation step before signing in.

### Missing or outdated information

Open the relevant screen and pull down to refresh where supported. Confirm that you are signed into the expected account.

### Account deletion

Open **Profile**, select **Delete account**, review the warning, and confirm. See the Account Deletion page for details.
