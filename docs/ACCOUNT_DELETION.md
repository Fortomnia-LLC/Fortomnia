# Delete Your Fortomnia Account

Fortomnia lets you permanently delete your account from within the app.

## Delete an Account in the App

1. Sign in to Fortomnia.
2. Open **Profile**.
3. Scroll to **Delete account**.
4. Review the permanent-deletion warning.
5. Confirm **Delete account**.

When deletion succeeds, you will be signed out and the account can no longer be used.

## Information Deleted

Account deletion is designed to remove your authentication account and associated Fortomnia records, including:

- Profile information.
- Workout sessions, exercises, sets, and notes.
- Workout templates.
- Nutrition entries and goals.
- Supplement protocols and adherence logs.
- Custom exercises owned by your account.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Limited records may be retained when legally required, and encrypted backup copies may remain temporarily until overwritten through normal backup processes.

## If You Cannot Delete Your Account

If you cannot sign in or the deletion process returns an error, contact `support@fortomnia.com`. Include the email address associated with the account and a description of the issue. Do not send your password or authentication codes.
